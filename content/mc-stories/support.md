MC Stories is an iOS app for motorcyclists — track each bike's maintenance, parts, costs, mileage, fuel, tyres and rides (recorded with GPS or imported from GPX), plus track days, photos and documents, all in one place.

------------

- Report a bug: email [mc-stories@12f.dk](mailto:mc-stories@12f.dk?subject=MC%20Stories%20bug%20report) with steps to reproduce, your iOS version, and your device model.

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device, and synced through your own private iCloud account. 12F has no servers and never sees your data. See the [privacy policy](/mc-stories/privacy/).

**Does recording a ride drain my battery?**
Recording uses GPS for the length of the ride, which does use power — the same as any ride- or run-tracking app. Location is only used while a ride is recording, never otherwise.

**I bought Pro but it's not showing on my other device.**
Open the Pro screen and tap **Restore purchase**. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, so it restores on any device signed in to the same account.

**How do I turn off analytics?**
**Settings → Share anonymous analytics**. It never includes any of your motorcycle data either way.

**Can I get my data out?**
Yes — Pro can export a bike's history as a PDF or CSV, produce a seller's document when you sell a bike, and hand a bike over to another rider as a transferable passport file.
